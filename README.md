### Now building: Panezo

**Two websites and your notes on one iPhone screen.** Keep a source above, write below, compare readable text and save selected findings locally.

- Resizable top/bottom split with two independent browser panes
- Scratch notes per pane and a searchable Notebook
- Saved workspaces that restore both pages and the layout
- On-device text comparison, with selected findings saved to Research
- Free, no ads, no analytics, no account. Label: *Data Not Collected*

Status: in TestFlight preparation for iPhone.
